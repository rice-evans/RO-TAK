RO-TAK

Plan together.

RO-TAK is an ATAK-style shared tactical map for Roblox. Draw, place units and take bearings, live with everyone in your room.

Draw the plan

Sketch routes with the pen, line and arrow. Box an objective, drop numbered markers and label what matters.

Place units

Friendly, hostile and neutral symbols in MIL-STD-2525 or APP-6. Pick the unit type, size and echelon and place it where it belongs.

Take bearings

Drag from one point to another to read the bearing from north.

Share a room

Pick a room code or send an invite link. Everyone sees the same map and marks as they change. No accounts.

Lead, and everyone follows

Lead the room and your view carries to anyone who taps Follow. Pan, zoom or rotate and they go with you.

Hide what you don’t need

Switch symbols, drawings, text, numbers or waypoints off for your own view. Everyone else’s map stays as it is.

And the rest

  • Grid coordinatesCalibrate the map once and read or jump to any grid reference.
  • Waypoints and timersNumbered waypoints with grid references, and countdown timers shared with the room.
  • Minimap and rotationRotate the map to your heading and keep the full picture in the corner when zoomed in.
  • Export and importCopy your marks as text and paste them into another room. Save the map as an image.
  • Rooms clean upA room is deleted after 24 hours with nobody in it.
  • Runs anywhereIn the browser, installed as an app on a phone, or as the desktop app for macOS and Windows.

Open source, all of it.

The website, the desktop app and the database rules are public on GitHub under the MIT license. Read the code, run your own copy or send a fix.

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.
  • Every desktop release is built on GitHub from the public code, with checksums and a signed build attestation.
  • Rooms are public, so don’t put anything private in one.
  • Found a problem? Report it privately through GitHub.

Check any download yourself:
