OPERATION ORDER 07-26

(TS//NF) On order, Task Force TEMPEST conducts an air assault from FOB EYE onto the northeastern island of the ARCHIPELAGO, clears the settlement designated OBJ GALE, and seizes the port facility designated OBJ STORM in order to capture the high-value target known by the alias ISOBAR and deny the COASTAL LIBERATION FRONT use of the harbor.

(S//NF) D-Day: 12 NOV 26. H-Hour: 0430L. Expected duration: 90 minutes.

1. SITUATION

a. Enemy Forces.

(S//NF) The COASTAL LIBERATION FRONT (CLF) holds the northeastern island with an estimated 40 to 55 fighters. Two rifle squads are garrisoned in the settlement at OBJ GALE, concentrated in buildings 1, 2 and 4. A mechanized section with one armored vehicle guards the port at OBJ STORM. A single squad patrols the ridge north of LZ ALPHA. One armed patrol boat operates east of the port.

(TS//NF) HUMINT from source KESTREL reports a MANPADS team on the roof of the northern warehouse at OBJ STORM since 07 NOV. The report is uncorroborated. All aircraft will treat the area north of PL LIGHTNING as a missile threat zone.

(S//NF) Most likely enemy course of action: CLF defends GALE in place and falls back to the port when contact is made, using the settlement to delay friendly forces while ISOBAR is moved to the harbor for extraction by boat.

(S//NF) Most dangerous enemy course of action: CLF engages inbound aircraft with MANPADS during the approach to LZ ALPHA and counterattacks the LZ with the ridge squad before friendly forces consolidate.

b. Friendly Forces.

(S//NF) Higher headquarters, JOINT TASK FORCE, conducts deception operations along the western coast from D-1 to draw CLF attention away from the northeastern island. Naval element SEAWALL blocks the eastern channel from H-30 and is responsible for the patrol boat.

c. Attachments and Detachments.

(S//NF) Two rotary-wing aircraft, callsigns DUSTOFF and RAVEN, are attached from AVN DETACHMENT for the duration of the operation. One armored section (1SEC) is attached from B COMPANY.

2. MISSION

(TS//NF) Task Force TEMPEST conducts an air assault NLT 120430L NOV 26 to clear OBJ GALE and seize OBJ STORM in order to capture ISOBAR alive and deny the CLF use of the northeastern harbor.

ALIVE. no exceptions — J.R.

3. EXECUTION

a. Commander's Intent. (S//NF) Speed and surprise. We are on the ground before the CLF can move ISOBAR to the water. The operation succeeds when ISOBAR is in our custody, the harbor is denied, and every element is back across PL THUNDER by H+90.

b. Concept of Operations.

(S//NF) Phase I, Air Assault (H to H+20). DUSTOFF inserts 1st Platoon at LZ ALPHA. RAVEN inserts 2nd Platoon at LZ BRAVO. Both aircraft fly the southern corridor below 200 ft and stay south of PL LIGHTNING. 1SEC crosses the land bridge at NAI 1 and holds the crossing.

(S//NF) Phase II, Clear OBJ GALE (H+20 to H+45). The weapons squad (WPN) establishes a support-by-fire position west of GALE and fixes buildings 1 and 2. 1st Platoon clears from the north. 2nd Platoon clears from the south through building 4. Building 3 is a no-fire area; suspected noncombatants.

(TS//NF) Phase III, Seize OBJ STORM (H+45 to H+70). 1st Platoon passes through GALE and assaults the port along the coastal road. 2nd Platoon isolates the port from the south. The assault element enters the command building (marked HVT on the overlay) and secures ISOBAR. Transmit EYEWALL on capture.

(S//NF) Phase IV, Exfil (H+70 to H+90). All elements move south by road along the green route to EXFIL. DUSTOFF lifts ISOBAR and any casualties from EXFIL directly to FOB EYE. Transmit LANDFALL when the last element crosses PL THUNDER.
